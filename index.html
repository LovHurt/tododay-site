TodoDay

A calm list for today

Write the day down, check things off, and let the rest roll over to tomorrow.

How it works

  • Add: type in the top line and press Return. Keep typing to add more.
  • Sub-item: swipe an item to the right. Swipe a sub-item right again to move it out.
  • Delete: swipe an item to the left. Tap Undo if you change your mind.
  • Reorder: long-press an item and drag it.
  • Tomorrow: unfinished items roll over on their own; finished ones are cleared overnight.
  • Language: TodoDay follows your iPhone's language. To choose a different one just for TodoDay, open Settings › TodoDay › Language.
